What's the difference between LiFePO4 and NMC batteries? ▼

LiFePO4 (LFP):

  • 3000-5000+ cycles (10+ year lifespan)
  • Safest lithium chemistry, no thermal runaway
  • Perfect for stationary and high-cycle applications
  • Slightly lower energy density

NMC:

  • 1000-2000 cycles (3-5 year lifespan)
  • Higher energy density, lighter weight
  • Best for performance and range applications
  • Requires more careful thermal management

Recommendation: LFP for cost-per-cycle, NMC for performance-critical applications.

Technical Support & Maintenance

How do I maintain my battery for maximum lifespan? ▼

For LiFePO4 Batteries (Recommended):

  • Charge regularly — avoid deep discharge below 20%
  • Avoid extreme temperatures (store in 15-35°C)
  • Use certified chargers only
  • Annual BMS health check recommended
  • Zero maintenance — no water top-ups needed

For Lead-Acid Batteries:

  • Check water levels monthly (if applicable)
  • Ensure proper ventilation
  • Keep terminal connections clean
  • Avoid over-discharge
What does "cycles" mean and why does it matter? ▼

A cycle is one complete charge-discharge-charge sequence.

  • LiFePO4 batteries typically offer 2000-5000+ cycles (10+ years daily use)
  • Lead-acid batteries offer 500-1000 cycles (2-3 years)
  • Higher cycle count = longer total lifespan = lower cost per year

Example: A 48V 100Ah LiFePO4 with 3000 cycles can deliver 14.4 MWh total energy over its lifetime, making it more economical than lead-acid despite higher upfront cost.

Can I use LiFePO4 batteries in my existing lead-acid system? ▼

In most cases, yes, but with considerations:

  • LiFePO4 voltage nominal is 3.2V/cell vs 2V/cell for lead-acid
  • Your charger must be LiFePO4-compatible
  • Some older controllers may need calibration
  • BMS protection is built-in with LiFePO4 (important safety feature)

⚠️ Important: Do NOT mix LiFePO4 and lead-acid batteries in the same system without technical validation. Contact our support team to verify compatibility before switching.

How do I troubleshoot battery issues? ▼

Common Issues & Solutions:

  • Low capacity: Check charger voltage, BMS health, temperature
  • Won't charge: Test charger output, verify connections, check BMS status
  • Rapid discharge: Possible internal short — stop use and contact support
  • Overheating: Reduce load, improve ventilation, check ambient temperature

First Step: Contact our technical support with battery model number and issue description. We'll guide you through diagnostics.
