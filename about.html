Who We Are

A young company with a clear focus

Arkaworld™ is a wholesale battery trading company based in Surat, Gujarat. We started with a simple goal — make it easier for dealers, mechanics, and small businesses to get genuine batteries at fair wholesale prices, without the hassle.

We stock batteries across 9 categories — bikes, cars, e-rickshaws, EVs, inverters, industrial, solar, energy storage, and e-cycles — from 20+ trusted brands including Exide, Amaron, Luminous, Okaya, and Livguard.

We're still growing. Every order matters to us, and we treat our customers like long-term partners, not transactions.
